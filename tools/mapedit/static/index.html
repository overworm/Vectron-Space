Hazard zones

Every block of connected lava / electro water / hidden wall tiles is a zone, numbered 1, 2, ... (shown on the map; hover a tile to outline its zone). A trigger switches off only the zone set in this field (0 = every zone of its kind). To change an existing trigger: pick its item tool and click on it (it takes the number in the field), or change the number right after placing / clicking it.

Level look

Applied to walls, floor, sprites, enemies and the boss of this level when the ROM is built. The preview here is approximate.

Floor set (paint floors per tile)

Liquids, sand, stone...: add up to 8 textures, then use "Floor paint" on the left. Walls and doors are not changed.

Wall textures (ids 1-9)

PNG or JPG, any size (made square and reduced to 16x16 texel classes by the build). Click a picture to choose another texture for that wall id.
